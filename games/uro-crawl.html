How to play

Clear the room, pick a door, grab the reward, repeat. A boss waits in room 9. Every instrument has a class; every monster has a family. Match them: bright pip over a monster = what hurts it, crossed pip = what it shrugs off.

A slam's red disc hurts and staggers you; the dashed ring past it only staggers (slowed, no attacking). Roll through the landing to shrug off both. Swap to the right tool and your first big hit gets a Right Tool bonus. Biofilm shields block 90% until stripped by water or cautery. Cutting makes things bleed; heat cauterizes. Freeze a stone, then shatter it.

Instrument aspects

An aspect is a pre-run variant of an instrument: it changes how the tool works, not just its numbers. Aspect I is the classic. Aspects II and III unlock at PGY-2 and PGY-4. Your picks apply to the starting Holmium and Scalpel and to a Loop or Trilogy you find on the way.
