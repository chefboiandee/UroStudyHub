WHAT TO TRY THIS ROUND

  • Two laser pedals: left DUST, right FRAG. Switch between them, even mid-lasing. Is it obvious which stone wants which?
  • Pinch points at the UVJ, iliac crossing and UPJ: fly through the ring, centred.
  • Irrigation vs the pressure gauge: enough flow to see, without going over 40 mmHg.
  • Basket a 2–4 mm fragment: no sheath, so it rides out to the bladder and back. Worth it?
  • Pull back when a fragment slips behind the scope (watch the fluoro).
  • Fire with the fiber on the wall and look for the burn marks and the PULS grade on the end screen.
  • Try both cameras (📷): chase cam vs scope view.
